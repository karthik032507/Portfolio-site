# Image prompts for Karthikeya's portfolio

How to use: open ChatGPT (GPT-4o image), Google Gemini, or any image tool that accepts a
reference photo. Upload `Linkedin DP.jpeg` as the reference, then paste one prompt at a time.
Save the results into this folder with the file names given.

If the result changes your face, reply with: "Keep my exact face from the reference photo,
only change the outfit and background." and regenerate.

## Image 3 — Footer / contact background  → save as `glow-bg.png`

Create a minimalist cinematic abstract background for a premium dark developer portfolio
website.

Use a 16:9 widescreen composition with a completely dark, almost pure-black background. The
upper 65–70% of the image should remain extremely dark and empty, creating large negative
space.

At the lower third of the composition, create a smooth abstract landscape-like glowing form:
a subtle neon green luminous wave / curved horizon emerging from the darkness.

The green glow should be concentrated around the bottom-center area, with the brightest point
slightly left of center. Create a smooth flowing curved line that rises gently toward the
center and then sweeps downward toward the right edge.

Use multiple layers of soft green atmospheric glow around the curve: deep dark green around
the outer glow, rich emerald green in the middle, bright neon lime green (#CCFF00) near the
central highlight, and a very subtle white-green highlight along the brightest curve.

The transition from green glow to black must be extremely smooth and atmospheric, with no
hard edges. The glowing curve should resemble a futuristic horizon or flowing energy wave,
but remain completely abstract.

No objects, no people, no text, no logos, no UI, no stars, no particles.

Style: premium cinematic technology aesthetic, futuristic, elegant, minimal, atmospheric,
soft volumetric lighting, smooth gradients, deep blacks, subtle neon green illumination.

Important: keep the upper portion almost completely black so foreground typography remains
highly readable. The green illumination must feel subtle and sophisticated rather than a
bright neon effect.

Aspect ratio 16:9, ultra-high resolution, seamless dark background.
